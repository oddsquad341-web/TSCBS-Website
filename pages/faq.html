Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with TSCBS.

How do I place an order?+

Simply browse our collection, click 'Add to Cart' on the products you want, then proceed to checkout. You can pay via UPI, credit/debit card, net banking, or choose Cash on Delivery.

Is Cash on Delivery available?+

Yes! COD is available for all orders across India. A small handling fee of ₹25 applies for orders below ₹499.

How long does delivery take?+

Metro cities: 2–3 days. Tier 2/3 cities: 4–5 days. Remote areas: 5–7 days. All orders are dispatched within 24 hours.

What is the return policy?+

We offer a 7-day hassle-free return policy. If you're not satisfied for any reason, WhatsApp us and we'll arrange a free pickup.

Are the products genuine?+

Absolutely! Every product is handpicked and quality-checked by our team before being listed. We work directly with trusted manufacturers.

Can I track my order?+

Yes! Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking link via WhatsApp/SMS. You can also use our Track Order page.

Do you offer bulk/wholesale orders?+

Yes, we offer special pricing for bulk orders. Contact us on WhatsApp at +91 99999 99999 with your requirements.

What payment methods do you accept?+

UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), Credit/Debit Cards, Net Banking, and Cash on Delivery.

Can I change or cancel my order?+

Orders can be changed or cancelled within 2 hours of placement. Contact us immediately on WhatsApp for the fastest response.
